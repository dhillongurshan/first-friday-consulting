Mr. Dhillon builds marketing functions that owners and CFOs can underwrite — engines measured in payback period and margin.

Result: gross margin up 14.2% to $21.4M on flat volume, and a go-to-market cost restructure that turned a $229K-loss acquisition channel into a $401K contributor, lifting return on new-business spend from $0.84 to $1.50 per dollar.

He built the pipeline machinery behind those numbers: a 115,000-contact B2B database converted into a predictable channel generating 20+ qualified leads a week at $58 each, plus a proprietary prospect-scoring tool that opened an entire equipment-intensive segment invisible to traditional field sales. The result: $17M in incremental revenue and monthly sales volume up 6x.

Before that, as Senior Communications Advisor to the Government of Alberta, he served as spokesperson for a major regulatory body through workplace incidents, international missions, and legislative overhauls — the training ground for advising boards under real pressure.
