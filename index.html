- For Reference

Points of Order: frequently asked questions

Why "Marketing by Committee"?

The name comes from the fact that most companies already run marketing by committee, whether anyone calls it that or not. Entry-level coordinators or managers navigating an owner's or president's vision. Or administrative staff supporting a half-baked marketing strategy from the side of their desk.

Marketing by Committee brings the horsepower to that committee. One expert, accountable to the group, running the function day-to-day. When a project needs more hands, our committee extends to a small network of vetted specialists, still run by one accountable principal.

What is a fractional CMO?

A fractional CMO is an experienced Chief Marketing Officer who works with your company part-time or on a defined engagement, providing executive-level marketing leadership without the cost or commitment of a full-time hire.

What does it cost?

A fractional CMO from Marketing by Committee costs $3,000 to $10,000+ per month, depending on the size of the company. That is roughly the same cost as hiring a marketing coordinator or manager in Alberta.

A full-time CMO in Canada costs $260,000 to $355,000 a year once you load salary, benefits, payroll taxes and recruiting fees.

Do you do the work, or only advise?

Both. I execute the marketing work and advise on it, and the difference matters. I have built the campaigns, written the copy, run the ad accounts and built the websites myself, so I know exactly what good work looks like before I hand anything off. AI tools let one operator now cover work that used to need a department, and I use those same tools daily. On a retainer, I execute directly, and when a project calls for a specialist, I bring one in from my network and stay accountable for the result. You deal with me. I deal with whoever's needed.

What is the difference between a fractional CMO and a marketing agency?

The difference between a fractional CMO and a marketing agency is accountability: an agency is accountable for activity, and a fractional CMO is accountable to the P&L. Agencies are hired to fill the top of the funnel. I am hired to decide whether that funnel should exist, what a customer is worth, and where the margin actually lives.

Most agency and in-house overhead exists because marketing used to require a standing department: a videographer, a web developer, a social media coordinator, a PR contact, a budget analyst. AI tools, including the ones behind this website, have collapsed most of that work into what one operator can run directly. When a scope genuinely needs more hands, I draw from a small network of vetted specialists instead of carrying their salaries year round, and I stay the one person accountable for what they produce. Often my first recommendation is about the agency you already have, and how much of its overhead you're still paying for.

What industries do you work with?

Marketing by Committee works with companies of all sizes, including small businesses, based in Edmonton, Alberta. My focus is energy, transportation, construction, forestry, mining, agriculture, fleet, retail, and professional services.

How many clients do you take?

Client capacity is limited because I run every engagement personally, and when a project needs a specialist the network gets called in under my direction, not a rotating account team. You always deal with the same person. When my capacity is full, new engagements start on a waitlist.

How long do engagements run?

Six months minimum is advised. Marketing that changes a revenue number does not do it in eight weeks, and any advisor who tells you otherwise is selling you a deck.

Where are you based?

Marketing by Committee is based in Edmonton, Alberta and advises companies across the Greater Edmonton Area, including Sherwood Park, St. Albert, Spruce Grove, Stony Plain, Beaumont, Nisku and Leduc.
