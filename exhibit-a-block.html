- Exhibit A -

The case for a fractional CMO.

If your company does $1 million to $10 million a year, you sit in a gap. Too big to leave marketing to whoever has time. Too small to justify a full-time CMO, a marketing department and an agency retainer.

Most companies in that range default to one of two things. Junior staff managing senior problems. Or an agency running the plan it sold you.

A fractional CMO closes the gap. An operator who has owned the P&L. Sets the strategy. Directs the agencies and the internal team. Reports to you monthly.

Executive leadership at a fraction of the cost of a full-time hire. You get the accountability. Not the overhead.
